    How a period works

    Each checked Gmail sends one job email to the next checked Gmail it is not connected to. One Gmail receives at most 3 warm-up emails in a period. If that Gmail already has 3, the sender emails another selected Gmail that is not connected yet and still has fewer than 3. The company name is Metaroom. The first email uses the built-in language templates, or your subject and body templates when that checkbox is on. The subject ends with (w-u), or with your custom index when that box is checked. If one Gmail hits an error, warm-up continues with the next Gmail. After the last email, the app waits 15 seconds, then each mailbox replies once for every warm-up email it got. Use my reply emails picks one saved reply at random. Leave that checkbox off to use a built-in reply. The reply search uses the same index, in:anywhere w-u unless you set a custom index. If a message is in Spam, the app clicks Report not spam after the reply. The Gmail that sent that email is marked Warming up. If that email was not in Spam, the Gmail that sent it is marked Active. One Spam message keeps that sending Gmail on Warming up. Later periods go to a Gmail that is not connected yet.

      Scheduled sequence

      After a client email is sent, step 1 and step 2 go out when their days have passed. A reply or a bounced address stops the sequence. A step with its own email keeps that text. An empty step uses a saved follow-up email when that checkbox is on, and otherwise uses the follow-up email above. The follow-up button still uses the sent date.

      Which emails get a reply

      The app searches that day's Sent mail for threads that still have only one message from you. A draft in that thread is deleted before the reply. Selected Gmails take turns, one follow-up each, until each reaches its Max. Once a search has no matches left, that Gmail is not searched again. If one Gmail hits an error, follow-up continues with the next Gmail.
